How to travel in Wales
Method one: Go on a train!
Wales has a comprehensive railway network that connects towns, cities, and rural areas. You can easily travel across the country, with major routes connecting cities like Cardiff and Swansea to West and North Wales, as well as cross-border services to England.
★ Note that there may be strikes or cancellations or delays would occur randomly to the train services but the services is overall reliable and there are many services everyday even if you missed a train , for more information and maps click here
Method two: Go on a Bus!
Wales has lots of types of bus services which they allow you to travel intercity with ease, featuring long distance buses and city buses that travels across wales, however it is difficult to travel from england to wales
★ There are many bus services that only occupy in specific areas in Wales, which means the service intervals would be very different. Therefore it is good to install a Welsh transport App (TrawsCymru) when you are traveling in wales by bus or you can follow the major dedicated bus companies TrawsCymru, Stagecoach and Fflecsi bus that operates across the whole Wales but note that they often have less services in Cities
